The Problem

Speed vs. Structure.

During lectures, students struggle to write quickly enough. Notes become chaotic and useless.

Current AI tools add friction: writing prompts, copy-pasting, switching tabs.

Michał

Student of Cognitive Technologies

Goal:

Deep understanding of the material, not just mindless transcription of the lecturer's words.

Frustration:

"I spend more time formatting and organizing notes than actually learning from them."

Target Audience

Designed for the Active Learner.

Students who handle large volumes of information and need to connect dots quickly.

They value efficiency and aesthetic minimal environments that aid focus.

User Journey Map

Stage Activity Pain Point Our Solution
During Lecture Quick writing to keep up. Chaotic notes, lack of structure. Raw Capture: Focus on content, leave formatting to AI.
After Lecture Reviewing and organizing. Time wasted on text editing. 1-Click AI: Instant structure and synthesis.
Before Exam Knowledge verification. Passive reading is ineffective. Active Recall: AI quizzes you on your notes.

Your Knowledge Hub

A strictly digital product designed to solve real problems.

  • Unified Interface
  • Dark mode optimized for focus
  • Instant access to synthesis & study modes

Intelligent Selection

Users don't need to manually collate information.

Simply select multiple notes (e.g., UX Design + Psychology) to create a powerful Synthesis.

No copy-pasting required.

Structured Notes

The app delivers complete, well-structured versions of your notes automatically.

Supports Markdown, clear hierarchy, and distinct sections for easy reading.

AI Without Prompts

Forget prompt engineering.

Access powerful tools like Quick Correction, Expansion, or Question Generation with a single click in the sidebar.

Custom prompts are available for advanced flexibility.

Active Studying

Notes aren't just for reading.

Use them as context for the AI Chatbot to:

  • Ask questions about specific lectures
  • Generate quiz questions
  • Verify your knowledge instantly
